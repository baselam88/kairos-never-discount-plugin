# Never-Discount Router

Routes every price pushback to a legal move: scope downsell, payment plan (same total), or back to objection handling when the real gap is belief.

A free plugin from KAIROS AI Systems — one focused tool that does one job well for a
small business: no suite to learn, no setup project, no account required beyond your
agent host.

## What it does

'Too much for everything' -> feature downsell at its real lower price. 'Want it, cash is the problem' -> payment plan, total invariant. 'Won't work' -> not a price problem. A plan that quietly lowers the total is a discount in costume — refused.

## How to use it

Add this plugin to a compatible agent host (Cursor, Grok Bot, or another
agent-plugins host), then ask in plain language, for example:

> Use the Never-Discount Router to help me: routes every price pushback to a legal move: scope downsell, payment plan (same total), or back to objection handling when the real gap is belief — I'll paste the details.

The plugin works from the facts you give it — your services, your prices, your
records. It never invents a number, a policy, or a result; anything it projects is
labelled an estimate with its basis. Drafts that would go to a customer stay drafts
until you approve them.
